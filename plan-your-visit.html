What Sunday feels like

A welcoming place to worship and grow.

You do not need to have everything figured out. Whether you are beginning your faith journey, returning to church, or looking for a church home, you are welcome here.

01

Arrive and settle in

Come in, find a seat, and take a moment to get comfortable. Members of our church family will be glad to help if you have a question.

02

Worship with us

Our service includes prayer, praise and worship, Scripture, and preaching from the Word of God. Participate at the level that feels comfortable for you.

03

Meet the family

After service, you are welcome to stay, say hello, and connect with our pastors and church family. There is no pressure and no expectation.

Come as you are

“You do not have to know all the words, understand everything, or pretend to be someone you are not. There is room for you here.”

Good to know

Questions first-time visitors often ask.

Do I have to dress up?+

No. Come in whatever helps you feel comfortable and ready to worship. You will see a range of styles on Sunday.

Will I be singled out?+

No. You are welcome to participate at your own comfort level. We want you to feel welcomed, never pressured.

Can I bring my children?+

Yes. Children, young people, and families are welcome to worship with us.

What if I need help with arrival or accessibility?+

Contact us before your visit and our church family will help you plan the easiest arrival for your needs.

Do I need to let someone know I am coming?+

Not at all. You can simply come on Sunday. If you would like us to expect you or answer a question beforehand, the optional form below is available.
