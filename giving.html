Giving

Giving is an expression of faith and gratitude.

Your generosity helps House of Bread Ministries share the gospel and serve people in practical ways in our community and beyond.

Why we give

Honor God with what He has entrusted to us.

We believe everything we have ultimately comes from God. Giving is one way we respond with gratitude, worship, and faith.

Proverbs 3:9–10 calls believers to honor the Lord with the firstfruits of their increase. Through faithful generosity, the church can feed, support, teach, reach, and serve.
